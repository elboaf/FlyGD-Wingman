Floating sig bar

A small always-on-top copy of the SIG / ROOT / NEXT readout that floats over other windows. Drag it anywhere on screen; it remembers where you left it. Its toggle also lives at the right end of the status strip.

Welcome to Wingman

An EVE multiboxing toolkit — client previews, bookmark keybinds and settings profiles — that also uploads your fight footage to YouTube.

Uploading is the half that needs a folder: Wingman watches one for new OBS recordings. Point it at the folder OBS saves to. The EVE tools do not need it, so you can set this up later in Settings.

Detect reads the folder from OBS’s own configuration.
